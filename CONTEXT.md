# The Loop

The Loop is a narrative horror game organized around stories of grief, fragmented memories, and recurring emotional stages.

## Language

**Story**:
A complete narrative composed of ordered loops and an eventual reveal.

**Story Loop**:
One titled passage through the story's grief stages.
_Avoid_: Level, chapter

**Stage Memories**:
The memories belonging to one grief stage within one story loop: maze memories plus one closing memory.
_Avoid_: Memory stack

**Maze Memory**:
A fragmented memory discovered in the maze before entering the active stage room.

**Closing Memory**:
The final memory viewed inside the active stage room after its maze memories are collected.
_Avoid_: Final memory

**Smoke**:
The embodiment of grief and suffering. It tries to engulf the player but is not evil or hateful.

**Maze Hunter**:
The smoke encountered in the maze. It creates sustained pressure and teaches a grief-stage-specific survival response.

**Room Guardian**:
The smoke encountered inside an active grief room. It makes the response learned in the maze dangerous, forcing the player to break that behavior before reaching the altar.

**Altar**:
The structure holding a grief room's Closing Memory.

**Story Reveal**:
The final revelation of a Story, reached through Acceptance rather than through an ordinary Closing Memory.
