# Smoke Behavior

The smoke represents grief and suffering. It is not evil and does not attack out of hatred. It only tries to engulf the player.

Each grief stage gives the smoke two complementary roles:

- **Maze Hunter:** creates sustained pressure and teaches the player a maladaptive survival response.
- **Room Guardian:** turns that learned response against the player, forcing them to break the behavior before reaching the altar.

Engulfment kills the player unless the stage explicitly defines otherwise. Acceptance is the intentional exception.

## Denial

### Maze Hunter

- The smoke advances only while it is inside the player's field of view.
- Outside the player's field of view, it flanks the player and tries to become visible again.

The maze teaches the player that refusing to look is safe.

The maze should provide loops, parallel paths, and short reconnecting routes so the smoke can circle the player without teleporting.

### Room Guardian

- The smoke advances while the player is not looking at it.
- Looking directly at it stops it or pushes it back.
- The player must face the smoke while moving toward the altar.

The room forces the player to stop avoiding grief and face it directly.

## Anger

This behavior is the current direction and may be refined.

### Maze Hunter

- The smoke pursues the player violently.
- Calm, slow, or evasive movement allows it to catch and kill the player.
- The player must rush directly at it to survive.
- When confronted, it flees to safety, repositions, and attacks from another angle.

The maze teaches aggression as a survival response while denying the player a satisfying confrontation.

### Room Guardian

- The smoke violently tracks the player while guarding access to the altar.
- Running toward it allows it to catch and kill the player.
- Calm movement makes it keep its distance.
- The player must calmly lure it away from the altar, then reach the altar without confronting it.

The room forces the player to relinquish the aggression that kept them alive in the maze.

## Bargaining

### Maze Hunter

- The smoke withdraws to expose an apparently safer route.
- After the player commits to that route, it repositions and applies pressure from elsewhere.
- It repeatedly encourages the player to second-guess decisions, change routes, and backtrack in search of a better outcome.

The maze teaches the player to believe that another choice can produce the perfect outcome.

The maze should provide branching routes, reconnecting paths, and believable alternatives rather than obvious dead ends.

### Room Guardian

- The room presents multiple routes to the altar.
- The smoke reacts strongly when the player reverses direction or changes routes.
- Committing to one route controls the encounter.
- Backtracking allows the smoke to close the distance.

The room forces the player to accept an imperfect choice instead of endlessly negotiating for a different outcome.

## Depression

### Maze Hunter

- The smoke moves only while the player moves.
- Remaining still stops it.
- Every attempt to make progress lets it creep closer.

The maze teaches the player that doing nothing is safe, even though stillness prevents all progress.

### Room Guardian

- The smoke advances while the player remains still or retreats.
- Moving deliberately toward the altar pushes it back.
- Sideways movement and circling do not create progress.

The room forces the player to abandon protective stillness and take deliberate forward action.

## Acceptance

Acceptance does not use the ordinary Room Guardian encounter.

### Acceptance Smoke

- The room initially appears to be an infinite expanse of sand with no blocks, walls, landmarks, or altar.
- The smoke follows at a constant distance while the player flees, making running meaningless.
- When the player stops fleeing, the smoke approaches and pauses just before contact.
- The player must take the final step and willingly allow it to engulf them.
- The engulfment is quiet, intimate, and nonlethal.
- The smoke dissolves, and only then does the altar materialize for the Story Reveal.

Acceptance is not the defeat of grief. The player embraces it, feels it, and continues without allowing it to control them.
