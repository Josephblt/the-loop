# Smoke Behavior

The smoke is the only antagonist.

It is shapeless smoke. It only tries to engulf the player. Its design should stay mechanically pure: hunting, tracking, closing distance, repositioning, and engulfing.

The smoke's behavior changes according to the current grief stage.

## Denial

Current locked behavior:

- The smoke gets closer only while it is inside the player's field of view.
- When outside the player's field of view, it tries to flank and become visible again.

Design intent:

Denial turns recognition into danger. Seeing the threat allows it to approach, but refusing to see it lets it reposition.

Required maze support:

- The smoke needs connected routes around the player.
- The maze should make it easy for the smoke to circle, flank, and re-enter the field of view without teleporting.
- The generator should bias toward loops, parallel paths, and short reconnecting routes.
