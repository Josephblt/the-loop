# Color Palette

The game's core palette uses blue, orange, and purple color families. Each
family has four brightness levels: deep, dark, medium, and light.

| Family | Deep | Dark | Medium | Light |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Blue | `#030C12` | `#071824` | `#397EAD` | `#C8ECFF` |
| Orange | `#140803` | `#281106` | `#C96724` | `#FFD6A3` |
| Purple | `#0C0515` | `#180B29` | `#75529E` | `#E1CCFF` |
