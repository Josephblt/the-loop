# Color Palette

The game's core palette uses blue, orange, and purple color families. Each
family has three brightness levels on a 1–10 scale, using stops 1, 6, and 10.

| Family | Dark · 1 | Medium · 6 | Light · 10 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Blue | `#071824` | `#397EAD` | `#C8ECFF` |
| Orange | `#281106` | `#C96724` | `#FFD6A3` |
| Purple | `#180B29` | `#75529E` | `#E1CCFF` |
