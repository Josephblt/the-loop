# Portal sound effects

Source: [Kenney Sci-Fi Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds)

Created and distributed by Kenney ([kenney.nl](https://kenney.nl)).
Licensed under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The portal effects use these source files:

- `pulsating_energy.wav`: converted from `engineCircular_002.ogg`.
- `energy_activation.wav`: converted from and reversed from `forceField_000.ogg`.
- `energy_deactivation.wav`: converted from `forceField_000.ogg`.

All files were converted to mono 44.1 kHz 16-bit PCM WAV.
